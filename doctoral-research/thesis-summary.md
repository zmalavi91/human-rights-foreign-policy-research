# Doctoral Research Summary

## Thesis

**Human Rights in International Relations: Role of USA and the European Union in Post-Cold War Era**

## Institution

Department of Political Science, Aligarh Muslim University, Aligarh, India.

## Research Period

May 2019 – June 2023.

## Research Focus

This doctoral research examined the role of the United States and European Union in shaping international human-rights norms, international frameworks, and geopolitical dynamics after the Cold War.

## Main Areas

- US foreign policy and human rights.
- EU foreign policy and human rights.
- Post-Cold War international order.
- Major-power intervention.
- Multilateral human-rights mechanisms.
- International norms and norm evolution.
- Global human-rights governance.
- Power, selectivity, and accountability.

## Methodological Approach

The research used qualitative and comparative historical analysis, supported by comparative case studies of foreign-policy practices, major-power interventions, multilateral institutions, and changing international norms.

## Academic Contribution

The research connected human-rights governance with foreign-policy behaviour, geopolitical interests, international institutions, and changing configurations of global power.

## Research Significance

The study contributes to debates concerning:

- Human rights and International Relations.
- Foreign-policy analysis.
- Global governance.
- International legitimacy.
- Humanitarian intervention.
- Accountability.
- Norm contestation.
- The relationship between principle and practice.
