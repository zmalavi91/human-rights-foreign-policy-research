# Research Methodology

## Research Design

The doctoral research used a qualitative and comparative research design.

## Methods

- Comparative historical analysis.
- Comparative case-study methodology.
- Foreign-policy analysis.
- Institutional analysis.
- Normative analysis.
- Literature synthesis.
- Policy analysis.
- Critical interpretation of official documents and scholarly literature.

## Sources

Potential source categories included:

- Government policy documents.
- International agreements.
- United Nations resolutions.
- Official statements.
- Parliamentary debates.
- Policy reports.
- Scholarly books and journal articles.
- International institutional records.
- Human-rights reports.
- Diplomatic statements.

## Analytical Focus

The analysis examined:

- Human-rights norms.
- Foreign-policy objectives.
- Strategic interests.
- International institutions.
- Geopolitical conditions.
- Intervention and accountability.
- Consistency between rhetoric and practice.
- Norm evolution.

## Research Ethics

The research process emphasizes accurate citation, responsible use of evidence, fair representation of political positions, protection of confidential information, and respect for copyright and publication restrictions.
